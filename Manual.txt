Failure to add or update items under the admin panel

1. Check the existing records in the following tables:

   * `menu_item`
   * `workflow_breakdown`
   * `role_workflow_breakdown`

2. Insert a new entry in the **`menu_item`** table.

3. Create a corresponding entry in the **`workflow_breakdown`** table.

4. Associate the **`workflow_breakdown`** record with the relevant **`menu_item`**.

5. Link the **`workflow_breakdown`** record to the appropriate role in **`role_workflow_breakdown`**.

6. Confirm that the `@role_required()` decorator in `routes.py` matches the **`id`** of the corresponding record in the `workflow_breakdown` table.

   * This allows the system to attach the workflow items to users based on their assigned roles during login.

**Notes**

* Ensure the new **menu item** is added to the navigation menu in `base.html`.
